## Declared limitations

- Model parameters are estimated on the full sample. State inference is causal (day *t* uses data up to *t* only), but the parameters themselves are in-sample.
- The regime-to-weight mapping in `strategy_engine_1.py` is derived from full-sample state statistics and is an in-sample interpretation step.
- The feature scaler is fit on the full sample.
- `gk_volatility` and `Range` are highly collinear (both derived from the same daily High/Low), which makes the full covariance matrices ill-conditioned.
- Index volume is reported as zero for much of the early history, so volume-based features were excluded.
